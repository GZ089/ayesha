Ayesha Mall
<p align="center"> <img src="https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/image/logo.png" alt="Ayesha Mall Logo" width="180"> </p>

AyeshaMall is an online shopping platform in Pakistan offering a curated selection of fashion, electronics, and home essentials.

Ayesha Mall is a private e-commerce and merchant management platform designed to help businesses create, customize, manage, market, and grow their online stores.

Private Project: Ayesha Mall is not open source. The project repository and source code are private and are therefore not uploaded to GitHub publicly.

Features
Store Overview

Get an overview of your own online store, including important store information and management options.

Store: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app

Shop

Manage and organize the products and shopping experience available in your store.

Shop: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/shop

Supplier Application

Fill out the supplier application to become a supplier and provide the required business information.

Supplier Application: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/supplierapplication

Authentication & Shop Settings

Create, customize, and manage shop settings through the authentication and account management system.

Authentication: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/authentication

Order Management

Manage customer orders and keep track of the order lifecycle from the store dashboard.

Orders: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/orders

Product & Inventory Management

Add, update, organize, and manage products while keeping track of store inventory.

Products: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/products

Business Analytics

Analyze business performance and gain insights into store activity, sales, and other important business metrics.

Analytics: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/analytics

Custom Domain

Configure and manage your own domain for your online store.

Shop Metadata: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/shopmetadata

Marketing

Use Ayesha Mall's marketing tools to promote products and grow your online business.

Ayesha Mall AI: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/ayeshamallai

Website Customization

Customize your own website and create a storefront that matches your brand and business requirements.

Ayesha Studio: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/ayeshastudio

Merchant Application

Apply to become an Ayesha Mall merchant and start selling through the platform.

Merchant Application: https://dropayesha.netlify.app/merchant/apply

Free Website Application

Apply for a free website that includes dynamic products, allowing your business to establish an online presence without building everything from scratch.

Free Website Application: https://ayesha-mall.netlify.app/sellman/apply
